## Technical Support

**App Name:** Bartending Simulator
**Developer:** Jason Xu

Thank you for using Bartending Simulator.
This page provides technical support information and help resources for users.

---

### 1. App Overview

Bartending Simulator is an offline bartending simulation app designed for learning and entertainment.
The app runs entirely on your device and does not require an internet connection.

---

### 2. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Does the app require an internet connection?**
A: No. Bartending Simulator works fully offline.

**Q: Does the app collect or transmit any personal data?**
A: No. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

**Q: Does the app use camera, microphone, or location services?**
A: No. The app does not access camera, microphone, or location.

**Q: The app crashes or does not open. What should I do?**
A: Please try the following steps:

1. Close and reopen the app
2. Restart your device
3. Update to the latest version of iOS
   If the problem continues, contact us using the information below.

**Q: How can I request a feature or report a bug?**
A: Please send a short description of the issue or suggestion to the support email below.

---

### 3. System Requirements

* iOS version: iOS 17.0 or later
* Supported devices: iPhone and iPad

---

### 4. Data & Privacy

Bartending Simulator respects user privacy.
The app does not collect personal data, does not track users, and does not include analytics or advertising services.

For more details, please refer to the Privacy Policy page.

---
